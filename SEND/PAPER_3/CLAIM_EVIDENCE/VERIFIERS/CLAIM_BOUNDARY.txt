The package preserves the Paper II/Paper III independent replication outputs
and their frozen provenance. A technical package self-containment PASS does
not by itself establish a new scientific claim. Existing scientific verdicts
and final status files are not rewritten by the packaging guard.

symlink, junction, or silent fallback. Original source locations may appear
only in provenance/history records.
