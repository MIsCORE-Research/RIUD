# Source-native waveform frequency versus Kerr QNM

Instantaneous absolute angular frequency was calculated from the unwrapped phase in fixed AhC-relative windows. The Kerr QNM real frequency uses source-native final spin and remnant mass; no fit or offset was introduced.

The comparison is a ringdown consistency check only. It does not identify the earlier surface transient as QNM excitation or establish the RIUD memory mechanism.
