# Horizon relaxation versus Kerr QNM timescale audit

For each SXS10 event, the first crossing of 50% and 90% of the AhC-to-final scalar change was read from the native `Horizons.h5` series. The comparison QNM scale is the source-native final-spin `(2,2,0)` damping time from qnm 0.4.4, converted to initial-M units with the preserved final-mass ratio. No relaxation fit or parameter tuning was performed.
- ArealMass: median tau50=0.972461M, median tau90=4.66332M; median QNM tau=11.7031M
- DimensionfulInertialSpinMag: median tau50=0.897197M, median tau90=5.8329M; median QNM tau=11.7031M
- chiMagInertial: median tau50=0.602684M, median tau90=4.17767M; median QNM tau=11.7031M

The crossing times are descriptive relaxation times, not a proof that the scalar horizon evolution is a QNM ringdown.
