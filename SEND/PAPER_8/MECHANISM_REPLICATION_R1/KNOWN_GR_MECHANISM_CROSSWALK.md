# MIsCORE known-GR mechanism crosswalk

This document compares completed MIsCORE/R8--R13 evidence with established
general-relativistic mechanisms. It is a theory crosswalk, not a new fit or
new scientific result.

## Interpretation

The closest physically coherent picture is a layered GR response:

```text
common-horizon formation
→ nonlinear angular/shear and curvature transient
→ horizon reorganization and absorption
→ distributed radiative history
→ late Kerr-QNM settling
```

This can motivate an effective age-structured description, because the state
of a newly formed horizon and the accumulated radiative/absorptive history
need not be summarized by a single instantaneous scalar. It does not prove
that the frozen RIUD S variable is one of these source observables, nor that
the R40 hazard is derived from Einstein's equations.

## Direct mapping to current boundaries

- R40 is an exact discrete/age-structured rate law within its frozen
  observational support.
- R7F2 tested GR instantaneous/history/kernel additions; the tested history
  and kernel did not recover the RIUD memory residual.
- R8 richer source-state additions did not close the R40 memory mechanism.
- R10 orbital/separation/spin support is incomplete across the topology.
- R11 common-horizon relaxation alone was not sufficient.
- R13 strict native-map support leaves post-reentry source support incomplete.
- Therefore the current evidence supports GR compatibility and a layered
  source-native candidate, but not a unique microscopic generator.
