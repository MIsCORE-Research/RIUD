# SXS10 horizon-derivative versus radiative-flux time-series correlation

Full native horizon and strain files were used. Horizon derivatives and sxs-derived radiative fluxes were interpolated onto a fixed 0.1M grid. Correlations and a prespecified ±20M/1M lag scan are descriptive; no coefficient, lag, or balance law was fitted. The 5–50M window is a boundary-exclusion control.
