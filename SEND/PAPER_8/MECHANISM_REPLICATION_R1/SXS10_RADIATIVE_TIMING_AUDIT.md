# SXS10 source-native radiative timing audit

Original Strain_N2.h5 files were loaded directly. Energy flux and angular-momentum flux were calculated with the installed sxs waveform routines. Peaks are independent maxima in the fixed AhC-relative 0–50M window. Scalar tau50/tau90 values come from the fixed horizon audit; no timing fit or event-specific selection was used.
- ENERGY_FLUX_PEAK: median peak tau=14.1203M; all scalar tau50 before peak=10/10; peak before h22=0/10; peak before h44=0/10
- ANGULAR_MOMENTUM_FLUX_NORM_PEAK: median peak tau=11.6707M; all scalar tau50 before peak=10/10; peak before h22=0/10; peak before h44=1/10

This establishes source-native temporal ordering only; it does not identify the flux peak with the observational GAP or the frozen RIUD transition.
