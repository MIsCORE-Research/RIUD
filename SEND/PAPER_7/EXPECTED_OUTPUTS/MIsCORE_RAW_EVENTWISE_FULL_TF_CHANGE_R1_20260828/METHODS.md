# Eventwise full-TF structural-change landmarks

H1 and L1 are scored separately. For each event and detector, C(t) is the sum of absolute left/right flank changes in robust power, phase-motion, and frequency-slope summaries over 20-2000 Hz. The strongest complete-support interior point is frozen per event/detector/width. Population averaging, t0-distance, GAP, MT, and node/path are excluded from selection. Only after selection is frozen, the corrected GAP_CENTER NATIVE_TAU table is joined for timing-error evaluation.
