Across 13 events, the direct cross-event complex-phase alignment survived an exact selector-aware conditional phase-randomization null. The observed equal-event zero-direction real component was 0.367157, the resultant length was 0.367704, and the mean phase was 0.054521 rad.

The primary null used 100,000 Monte Carlo draws after rerunning the exact path selector on 200 real H1/L1 off-source control arrays per event, for 2,600 selector executions. No draw exceeded the observed statistic, giving a plus-one empirical p-value of 9.9999e-06. The observed statistic exceeded the maximum primary-null realization (0.341506; 99th percentile 0.163338).

The joint path-existence null also produced zero exceedances in 100,000 draws (p = 9.9999e-06).

This rejects the specific explanation that the observed cross-event phase alignment is generated solely by favorable path selection under the implemented selector-aware conditional null.

Claim boundary: this is not an empirical off-source detector-phase null because the off-source assets contain real H1/L1 selection arrays but not empirical complex detector phases. It does not establish a physical origin, a violation of general relativity, or quantum gravity.
